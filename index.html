How TapOnce works

One quick tap. Five steps behind the scenes.

01

Issue

The university signs an anonymous student credential.

02

Challenge

The merchant creates a fresh one-time sale challenge.

03

Tap

The student wallet signs the challenge and discounted amount.

04

Verify

The terminal checks signatures, expiry, replay and spending cap.

05

Settle

Queued demo transactions are processed by the local settlement simulator.
